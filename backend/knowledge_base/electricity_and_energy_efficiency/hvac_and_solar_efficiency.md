# Commercial and Campus Building Energy Conservation & HVAC Efficiency

**Source:** Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) & Energy Conservation Building Code (ECBC)  
**Category:** Electricity and Energy Efficiency  
**Publication Date:** 2024-01  

## 1. HVAC Cooling Setpoint Standards
HVAC and cooling equipment represent 40% to 65% of institutional electrical consumption in commercial and university campus buildings.
The Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) mandates a default air conditioning setpoint of **24°C to 26°C**.
- Each 1°C increase in AC temperature setpoint reduces total chiller and split-unit electrical consumption by **6% to 8%**.
- Implementing automated programmable schedules to shut down AHUs (Air Handling Units) 30 minutes before occupancy closes prevents parasitic night baseload drain.

## 2. Lighting and Smart Building Automation
- **LED Retrofit:** Transitioning from T5/T8 fluorescent tubes to high-efficacy LED fixtures yields an average **45% to 55% reduction** in lighting electricity demand.
- **Occupancy Sensors:** Installing infrared occupancy sensors in lecture halls, laboratories, and administrative rooms reduces lighting run hours by 20% to 35%.
- **Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs):** Equipping central water-cooled pumps and fans with VFDs matches motor speed to actual thermal loads, cutting pump energy usage by up to 30%.
