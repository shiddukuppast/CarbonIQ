# Central Electricity Authority (CEA) CO2 Baseline Database & Grid Decarbonization

**Source:** Central Electricity Authority, Ministry of Power, Government of India (Version 19.0)  
**Category:** Electricity and Energy Efficiency  
**Publication Date:** 2024-03  

## 1. Indian Power Grid Baseline Emission Factors
Under the Indian National Grid standards published by the Central Electricity Authority (CEA), Scope 2 indirect carbon emissions from grid-purchased electricity are calculated using the national average weighted grid emission factor. For the regional unified grid, Version 19.0 of the CO2 Baseline Database establishes the weighted average emission factor as **0.82 kg CO2e per kWh** (including transmission and distribution network adjustments).

## 2. Decarbonization Trajectories & Clean Energy Integration
Facilities consuming power from the Indian unified electrical grid can directly reduce their Scope 2 emissions through:
- **On-Site Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Generation:** Rooftop solar installations deliver zero marginal operating emissions (0.00 kg CO2e/kWh), displacing grid imports during daytime peak tariff intervals.
- **Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs) & Open Access PPA:** Procuring power through Green Energy Open Access allows institutions to contract clean power directly from wind and solar farms.
- **Power Factor Correction:** Maintaining a power factor above 0.98 reduces reactive power losses, lowering net active power draws and line heating losses across campus transformers.
