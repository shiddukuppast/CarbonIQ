# GHG Protocol Corporate Standard: Scope 1 Fleet Mobility & Fuel Emission Factors

**Source:** World Resources Institute (WRI) & World Business Council for Sustainable Development (WBCSD)  
**Category:** Transportation and Sustainable Mobility  
**Publication Date:** 2023-11  

## 1. Scope 1 Direct Mobile Combustion Accounting
Direct emissions from owned or leased institutional vehicle fleets (diesel buses, petrol maintenance vehicles, vans) fall under Scope 1 mobile combustion.
Standard IPCC and GHG Protocol emission factors per unit of fuel consumed:
- **Commercial Diesel:** **2.68 kg CO2e per liter** consumed.
- **Motor Gasoline (Petrol):** **2.31 kg CO2e per liter** consumed.
- **Compressed Natural Gas (CNG):** **2.75 kg CO2e per kg** consumed.

Emissions are strictly calculated either via the Fuel-Based Method (liters consumed multiplied by the fuel emission factor) or the Distance-Based Method (kilometers traveled multiplied by average vehicle efficiency and fuel factor). The fuel-based method provides the highest empirical accuracy.

## 2. Electrification & Fleet Decarbonization Pathways
Replacing conventional 40-seater diesel commuter buses with Electric Buses (e-Buses) eliminates direct Scope 1 tailpipe emissions. While electric vehicles shift emissions to Scope 2 (grid electricity consumed for charging), the overall lifecycle emission reduction exceeds **55% to 70%** under standard grid mixes and up to **100%** when recharged with on-site solar PV generation.
