# IPCC Waste Model & Landfill Methane Avoidance Accounting

**Source:** Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) Guidelines for National Greenhouse Gas Inventories  
**Category:** Waste Management and Circular Economy  
**Publication Date:** 2023-09  

## 1. Landfill Methane Generation and Emission Factors
Municipal solid waste disposed of in open dumps or unmanaged landfills decomposes anaerobically, generating fugitive methane (CH4) gas. Methane possesses a Global Warming Potential (GWP) 28 times greater than carbon dioxide over a 100-year timescale.
- **Landfill Disposal Factor:** Commingled unsegregated municipal solid waste disposed in conventional landfills generates an estimated **0.55 kg CO2e per kg of waste**.
- **Methane Generation Potential (Lo):** Degradable Organic Carbon (DOC) in food waste, paper, and landscape pruning drives methane output.

## 2. Circular Economy Avoided Emission Offsets
Under IPCC and GHG Protocol Scope 3 Category 5 guidelines:
- Diverting organic biodegradable waste from landfills to aerobic composting or biomethanation processes avoids anaerobic methane formation.
- Composting yields an avoided methane offset credit of **-0.25 kg CO2e per kg of organic waste diverted**.
- Materials recovery and recycling (metals, plastics, cardboard) yield an avoided virgin extraction offset credit of **-0.45 kg CO2e per kg recycled**.
- **Important Carbon Accounting Principle:** These negative emissions represent an avoided greenhouse gas credit (avoided future baseline release), distinct from technological carbon capture or biogenic sequestration.
