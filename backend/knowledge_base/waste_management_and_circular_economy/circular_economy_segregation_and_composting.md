# Solid Waste Management Rules & Circular Economy Recovery Practices

**Source:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) Solid Waste Management Rules  
**Category:** Waste Management and Circular Economy  
**Publication Date:** 2024-01  

## 1. Source Segregation Protocol
Effective circularity begins with strict three-stream segregation at source:
- **Wet Biodegradable Stream (Green Bins):** Food scraps, cafeteria organics, tea grounds, garden waste.
- **Dry Recyclable Stream (Blue Bins):** Paper, cardboard, packaging films, PET plastic bottles, aluminium cans.
- **Domestic Hazardous & E-Waste (Red Bins):** Batteries, fluorescent tubes, broken electronics, lab chemicals.

Mixing organic waste with recyclables contaminates dry paper and cardboard fibers, lowering recycling yield by up to 60% and redirecting recoverable resources to landfills.

## 2. On-Site Composting & Circular Diversion Targets
- **Campus Composting:** On-site aerobic composting pits or mechanical in-vessel composters convert dining hall organic waste into nutrient-rich soil humus within 21 to 30 days.
- **Diversion Rate Benchmark:** Institutional zero-waste certification targets achieving a minimum **75% waste diversion rate** from landfill disposal through combined composting and verified vendor recycling partnerships.
